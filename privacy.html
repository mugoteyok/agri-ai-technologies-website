1. Who we are

Agri AI Assist is operated by Nexvora Technologies SMC Limited, Uganda. Website: agri-ai-technologies-website.pages.dev. Privacy contact: mugoteyokana@gmail.com. We decide why and how personal information is processed in the app and act as the data controller under Ugandan law.

2. Information we collect

Account and contact information

  • Name, email address and phone number, where provided.
  • Sign-in and account details, and account type (farmer, supplier or agricultural business).

Agricultural and farm information

  • Farm or business profile, crop types and farming activities.
  • Approximate location (district) where you enter it, or precise device location only if you enable a location feature and give permission.
  • Crop disease questions, diagnosis history, and coffee grading submissions and results.

Images and submissions

  • Photos of crops, leaves, coffee beans or other agricultural material, and the questions, descriptions and messages you send to the app’s AI features.

Marketplace information

  • Produce and input listings, descriptions, photos and prices.
  • Seller details, orders, enquiries, transaction records and related messages.

Technical and usage information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, diagnostics, IP address and how you use the app.
  • Advertising identifiers, where ads are enabled.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage accounts and profiles.
  • Provide AI agricultural guidance, crop disease identification and coffee grading.
  • Operate marketplace listings, enquiries and orders.
  • Recommend agricultural products and suppliers, and develop market intelligence features.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Keep the app secure and prevent misuse.
  • Measure performance and improve our services.
  • Show and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Artificial intelligence and image processing

When you submit a question, description or image for AI analysis, it may be sent to an external AI provider, currently Google Gemini, which generates the response. Images and text are used to provide the feature you requested, and we do not use them to identify you personally. The provider processes this information under its own privacy policy and data-processing terms.

AI-generated information is for guidance only. It may be inaccurate or incomplete and is not a substitute for inspection or advice from a qualified agronomist or other professional. Use the report option in the app to flag any incorrect or harmful result.

5. How we share information

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database and file storage.
  • Render: hosting of our backend services.
  • Google Gemini: AI processing of questions and images when the AI feature is used.
  • Google AdMob: ad delivery and measurement, where advertising is enabled. Advertising identifiers and device information may be shared with AdMob.
  • Payment providers, where payments are enabled, to process or verify transactions under their own policies.

Marketplace users

Anything you put in a marketplace listing, including descriptions, photos, prices and seller contact details, may be visible to other users. Share only what you are comfortable making public.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose information where required by law, legal process or a competent authority.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. International data processing and transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process your information outside Uganda. Our recorded hosting regions are Supabase EU West (Ireland) and Render Ohio (United States). Google Gemini and AdMob may process data in other countries, depending on their services and configurations. Where personal data is transferred outside Uganda, we take steps to apply safeguards and protections consistent with applicable Ugandan data protection law.

7. Data retention

  • Account and profile data: kept while your account is active. Verified account deletion requests are processed within 30 days, subject to records that must be retained by law or for legitimate security purposes.
  • Crop images, AI questions and grading results: kept for up to 12 months, or until you delete your account, whichever occurs sooner, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Marketplace listings: kept while active and, where necessary, for up to 12 months after removal.
  • Orders and transaction records: retained for up to 5 years where required for accounting, tax, legal or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Diagnostics and security logs: retained for up to 12 months, where collected.
  • Backups: deleted or overwritten within 90 days under the applicable backup cycle, where supported.

We do not keep personal information longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

8. Information security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS between the app, our servers and our service providers. We also use access controls, authentication and other safeguards suited to the information we process. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy rights

Under Ugandan law you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion where legally permitted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at pdpo.go.ug.

To exercise these rights, email mugoteyokana@gmail.com. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations.

10. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Settings > Delete Account, or without the app at our account deletion page. We process verified requests within 30 days. Deletion removes your profile, uploaded images, AI history and marketplace listings, except records we must keep by law or for security and fraud prevention.

11. Advertising and consent

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent before processing this data. You can also manage advertising preferences, including resetting or opting out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

12. Children’s privacy

Agri AI Assist is intended for adults involved in agriculture and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information without the authorisation required by law. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will review it.

13. Third-party services and websites

The app may link to third-party sites and services with their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices, so please review their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our app, providers or legal obligations change. We will update the “Last updated” date and, where required, notify you or ask for consent to material changes.
