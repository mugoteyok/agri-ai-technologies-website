2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Agri AI Assist, we may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Account and Contact Information

  • Name and account identification details.
  • Email address and telephone number, where provided.
  • Authentication and account-related information.
  • Account type, such as farmer, supplier or agricultural business.

2.2 Agricultural and Farm Information

  • Farm and agricultural business profile information.
  • Crop types and farming activities.
  • District or general location information where provided.
  • Crop disease questions, diagnosis history and related information.
  • Coffee grading submissions and related results.

2.3 Images and User Submissions

When you use agricultural diagnosis or coffee grading features, you may submit photographs of crops, leaves, coffee beans or other agricultural materials.

We may also process the questions, descriptions, messages and other information you submit when interacting with the application's AI features.

2.4 Marketplace Information

If you use marketplace features, we may collect information relating to:

  • Produce and agricultural input listings.
  • Product descriptions, photographs and prices.
  • Supplier and seller information.
  • Orders, enquiries and transaction-related records.
  • Communications relating to marketplace activities.

2.5 Technical and Usage Information

We and our service providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application version and diagnostic information.
  • IP address and connection-related information.
  • Application usage and interaction information.
  • Advertising identifiers and related information where applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for purposes connected with operating, improving and securing Agri AI Assist, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing agricultural information and AI assistance.
  • Supporting crop disease identification and diagnosis guidance.
  • Providing coffee grading and related agricultural services.
  • Managing farmer, supplier and agricultural business profiles.
  • Operating marketplace listings, enquiries and orders.
  • Providing agricultural product and supplier recommendations.
  • Developing agricultural market intelligence features.
  • Responding to support requests and user enquiries.
  • Maintaining application security and preventing misuse.
  • Understanding application performance and improving our services.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements where applicable.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Image Processing

Agri AI Assist uses artificial intelligence to provide agricultural assistance and related features.

When you submit a question, description or image for AI-powered analysis, the relevant information may be transmitted to an external AI service provider for processing and generation of a response.

These services may help us provide:

  • Crop disease identification and guidance.
  • Agricultural questions and answers.
  • Recommendations relating to farming inputs and treatments.
  • Coffee bean analysis and grading assistance.
  • Other AI-supported agricultural services.

Information submitted to an AI provider may be processed according to that provider's own privacy policies, terms and applicable data-processing arrangements.

AI-generated information is provided for assistance and informational purposes. It may not always be accurate or complete and should not be treated as a substitute for professional agricultural inspection or expert advice.

5. How We Share Information

We may share personal information with service providers and other parties where reasonably necessary to operate Agri AI Assist, provide our services or meet legal obligations.

5.1 Technology and Infrastructure Providers

Our application uses third-party technology providers that may process information on our behalf. These include:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, file storage and related application infrastructure.
  • Render: Hosting and operation of backend application services.
  • Google Gemini: Artificial intelligence processing for supported agricultural features.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Advertising delivery and measurement where advertisements are enabled.

5.2 Payment and Transaction Providers

Where payment functionality is enabled, relevant information may be shared with payment service providers to process or verify transactions.

Payment providers may independently process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5.3 Marketplace Users

Information you choose to include in marketplace listings may be visible to other users of the application. This may include product descriptions, photographs, prices and seller contact information.

Please consider carefully what information you make publicly available through marketplace features.

5.4 Legal and Regulatory Disclosures

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, a valid legal process or a competent regulatory authority.

Nexvora does not sell personal information as an ordinary business activity.

Some of our technology and service providers may process or store information outside Uganda.

Our current infrastructure arrangements include Supabase and Render. The hosting regions currently identified in our internal infrastructure records are provisional and remain subject to independent verification.

Other service providers, including AI and advertising providers, may process information in additional countries depending on their infrastructure and service arrangements.

Where personal information is transferred or processed outside Uganda, we will take applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards into account.
