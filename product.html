Agri AI Assist

Intelligent Agriculture. Better Decisions. Stronger Markets.
Agri AI Assist brings artificial intelligence, agricultural intelligence and digital marketplaces together in one mobile experience.

01

AI Disease Detection

AI-powered crop analysis designed to help identify potential plant diseases and provide relevant agricultural guidance.

02

Market Intelligence

Tools designed to help users understand prices, price movement, trends and market conditions.

03

Produce Marketplace

A digital marketplace where farmers can list produce and buyers can discover available agricultural products.

04

Farm Supplies

A marketplace component connecting farmers with suppliers offering relevant agricultural products and supplies.

From detection to action

Connecting information with practical next steps.

A crop image can lead to AI analysis, potential disease identification, agricultural guidance and relevant supply recommendations.

From information to markets

Market information can connect agricultural price signals with a user's consideration of when and where to sell produce.

Technology behind the platform

Agri AI Assist is built using Flutter for the mobile application, FastAPI for backend services, Supabase for core cloud capabilities and machine-learning/AI services for intelligent features. Mobile-money capabilities are being developed to support relevant marketplace transaction flows.

Responsible AI

AI outputs are intended as decision-support information and may be incomplete or inaccurate. They should not automatically be treated as professional agricultural advice.
