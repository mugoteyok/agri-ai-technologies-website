About us

Technology for a smarter agricultural future.

NEXVORA TECHNOLOGIES SMC LIMITED is an agricultural technology company building practical digital solutions at the intersection of artificial intelligence, agricultural data and digital markets.

Who We Are

We combine software engineering, artificial intelligence and agricultural technology to develop digital products designed around real agricultural workflows.

Our Mission

To build practical technology that makes agricultural knowledge, intelligence and markets more accessible.

Our Vision

A smarter, more connected agricultural ecosystem powered by technology.

Built from Uganda. Designed for Possibility.

We are building from Uganda with a focus on solutions that can serve agricultural ecosystems across Africa. African agricultural systems are diverse, so our products are being developed with adaptability and local context in mind.

Our Approach

Practical

Technology should address identifiable needs.

Connected

Information, people and markets should work together.

Intelligent

AI should be applied where it creates meaningful value.

Our Flagship Platform

Agri AI Assist brings AI-powered crop analysis, market intelligence and marketplace capabilities into one mobile experience.
