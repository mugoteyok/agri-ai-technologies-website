Delete your Agri AI Assist account

You can permanently delete your Agri AI Assist account through the mobile application. If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by contacting Nexvora Technologies.

How to request account deletion

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Agri AI Assist and sign in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Delete Account.
  3. Review the deletion warning and confirm your request.
  4. The app securely sends your authenticated deletion request to the Agri AI Assist account-deletion service.
  5. When the deletion process succeeds, your account is deleted and your app session is cleared.

You must be signed in to use this option. If your session has expired, sign in again before requesting deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the application, email us from the email address associated with your account. If you registered using a phone number, include that number so we can locate and verify your account.

Email a deletion request

Contact: mugoteyokana@gmail.com

We will verify that the request belongs to you before processing it. Verified email requests are processed within 30 days.

What happens to your data?

When an authenticated deletion request succeeds, the account-deletion service removes the account and associated records managed by the service, including:

  • Supabase authentication account and user profile records.
  • Farmer profile and related account information.
  • Wallet, withdrawal and transaction records handled by the deletion service.
  • Marketplace product listings, promotions and order records handled by the service.
  • Crop disease history and associated disease-scan files.

Information held independently by third-party providers, or records subject to separate legal retention requirements, may require separate handling. Deleting your account does not automatically delete information already shared with other marketplace users.

Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your account and associated application records. If the deletion request fails, the app will need to report the failure rather than treating the account as successfully deleted.

Partial deletion

If you want specific information removed without closing your account, email us and identify the information you want us to review.
